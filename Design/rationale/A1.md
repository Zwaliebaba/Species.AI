# Rationale A1: world and regions

> Status: Final · Area: A1 · Documents: [World and regions](../gdd/01_world_and_regions/README.md) ([world and sites](../gdd/01_world_and_regions/world_and_sites.md), [terrain, time and views](../gdd/01_world_and_regions/terrain_time_and_views.md), [skirmish maps and map tools](../gdd/01_world_and_regions/skirmish_maps_and_map_tools.md)) · Ledger: [08 maps and level design](../gdd/adoption_ledger/08_maps_and_level_design_ledger.md) · Related: [owner decisions](../decisions/owner_decisions.md) O38 to O40 · [rationale index](README.md)

This file keeps the reasons behind the A1 design: the weakest point that the challenge found, every finding and how it was resolved, the alternatives that were rejected, the recommendations, the model results and the owner decisions taken. One challenge round was run (22 findings: 0 Critical, 5 High, 12 Medium, 5 Low; 8 improvements). The revision passed 120 KB and split the document into a folder of three files and an index; every requirement id of the draft was kept.

## Weakest point

The draft designed and measured the world only at the moment of its creation. Every density, contact and equality figure came from a fresh world that stewards filled in ranked order, from one seed, and the model had no clock. A persistent world does not stay in that state, and the draft's own rules decided what it became: a border area went to the first settlement whose territory reached it, so AI settlements and the launch cohort held the land between them and every later joiner; a dormant steward kept a place for 42 world days, so a busy world of 200 refused 147 of 895 joiners in a year while 87 sites stood empty; and with the AI count and the sites fixed for ever, a weakly settled world fell to 1.4 settlements per region with no steward beside another. The geometry was sound; its allocation over time was neither designed nor modelled. The revision answers it in the design (border areas split between their two sides, no place held for the dormant and a return queue, stand-in AI settlements kept by a keeper, world constants recorded at creation) and in the model (a `lifetime` mode that runs a world for a year of world days with joining, leaving, dormancy under O34 and returns, over five seeds per scenario, and layout figures over ten seeds).

## Alternatives rejected

| Choice | Rejected | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Terrain approach | Procedural from the reference algorithm alone | Nothing would guarantee level sites, open channels, dry crossings, equal resources or reachability; method 2's bias would spread over a continent |
| Terrain approach | Authored worlds per capacity band | Beyond a small team at 3 to 98 regions; every server would play the same solved map; a fixed world cannot follow a capacity raise |
| Terrain runtime | Generating on every client at load | The terrain never changes after creation; baked chunks with digests make the server the owner and spare every client the generator's floating-point reproducibility |
| Region borders | Ridges with passes (the draft) | The adopted movement rules let crowds walk over a ridge (A1-F03); channels need no new movement rule |
| Site jitter | Per-site jitter of 128 wu (the draft) | Contact days from 3.0 to 7.7 and home cells differing by 30 % (A1-F07) |
| Holding land | First to reach the halfway line holds the whole lens (the draft); unheld until a claim settles it | The first makes arrival order permanent (A1-F01); the second delays every contact behind a claim |
| Places of the dormant | A place held for 42 world days (the draft); a return margin of extra sites | Late joiners refused while sites stand empty (A1-F02); the site margin already holds the stand-ins and serves returns |
| AI settlements | A fixed count only; diagonal pairs in central valleys (A1-M2); valleys kept free of AI for steward groups | Thin worlds lose their neighbours; empty valleys on day 1; a solo steward without neighbours |
| The warning | A forecast at the estimated arrival (the draft); a warning radius (A1-M7); a world sparse enough that travel takes 10 minutes | Beaten by speed effects; adds a value for no gain; settlements 12,000 wu apart never meet |
| March speed | B4's formation speed of 12 wu/s | Every travel limit fails; the diameter of a world of 200 would be 118 to 127 minutes |
| Battle load | Handing A2 the base case alone (the draft); sizing for the stress case without caps | The low end of plausible inputs (A1-F05); a budget of 28 battles at 200 and 12 on an integrated host |
| Flatten for plateaus and bridges | The reference Absolute type only; a ramp type that interpolates across its square | Rim steps of 31 to 52 wu; one feathered level square serves cores, centres and bridges alike |
| Lifetime | Unlimited everywhere; 28 world days; eras in v1 (A1-M6) | No arc or fresh start for worlds of strangers; shorter than the plateau plus a month; the owner chose no eras (O39) |
| Stage-2 capacity | Up to 50; host-configurable up to 200 | Needs A2's dedicated budget before stage 3 (O40) |
| Map editor (DEC-045) | A player-facing editor in v1; no editor ever | Modding is a vision non-goal; world templates and set pieces need a tool later |
| Time of day and world weather | A day and night cycle; weather that hurts or pushes units | Changes the reference look and rewards set hours; an unannounced loss while away breaks PIL-049 |

## Recommendations

**Adopted:** island valleys with sea channels and crossings; split border areas and claims bound to own borders, with a 17-unit bound for PIL-028; stand-in AI settlements and the keeper (O38); the founding ranking with a room weight, "found beside" and groups; no held places and a return queue; the warning gate; battle caps with waiting attacks (values for A2 at gate B); the feathered flatten type with two-pass generation; the reserved extent and recorded world constants; reproducible worlds from a seed with regeneration on validation failure; the world calendar that contains the pace; the final-day attack close; the eight decisions of the triage, resolved on 2026-10-18 (DEC-045, DEC-315, DEC-324, DEC-562, DEC-802, DEC-865, DEC-866, DEC-966).

**Rejected or passed on:** compact AI placement (A1-M2); AI-free valleys; eras in v1 (O39, passed to B5 for stage 2); a warning radius; a frontier that opens regions from the centre outwards (it would conflict with the extension order); a day and night cycle; weather hazards in worlds; per-biome rules; carrying strength into a successor world.
