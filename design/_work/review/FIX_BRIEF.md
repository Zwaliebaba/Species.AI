# Fix-pass brief — Phase 5

Eight independent reviewers (`_work/review/R1.md` … `R8.md`) have verified a seeded sample of claims against the source code and listed:
- wrong or partly-correct claims;
- implementability gaps;
- cross-document inconsistencies;
- acceptance-check failures.

Your job is to apply the fixes to the documents you own.

## Rules

1. **Own only your documents.** Edit only the final documents assigned to you, plus any `_work/scripts/S_<NN>_*.py` generators that produce their tables. If a fix needs a change in a document you don't own, record it in your fix log under "Requests for other documents"; the coordinator routes it.
2. **Verify before you change.** Reviewers can be wrong too. For every finding that touches your documents, open the cited code or data and decide:
   - **Accept:** apply the fix;
   - **Reject:** keep the text and cite the evidence;
   - **Partly accept.**

   Never apply a correction you have not confirmed. Where two reviewers disagree, the code decides.
3. **Find every finding that concerns you.** Grep all `R1.md`–`R8.md` for your document numbers (e.g. `02_`, `| 02 |`, `02 §`, `02:`). The cross-document sections of other reviewers name your document too. Also apply the coordinator verdicts below.
4. **Fix fully.**
   - Correct the claim and its tag, and every other place in your document that repeats it: tables, the summary, the closing sections, and any Mermaid diagram.
   - Fill each implementability gap with the missing constant, definition or transition, verified in code.
   - If a reviewer shows that one of your open questions (`NN-Ux`) is already resolved, close it with the resolution and evidence.
   - Keep the document's structure, its [C]/[I]/[U] discipline and its `path:Symbol` citation format.
5. **Final documents must be self-contained.** Do not cite `_work/` drafts, CSVs or scripts as the *evidence* for a claim. Cite the source code or data. A pointer such as "generated by `_work/scripts/…`" is fine as provenance. Where a document says "see `_work/manifest.csv`" in place of a list the spec requires, inline the list.
6. **Re-run the checks** from `_work/SYNTH_BRIEF.md` "Mandatory pre-finish checks" (Mermaid validator and citation check) until your documents are clean.
7. **Write a fix log** at `_work/review/F_<YOUR_ID>.md` with these parts:
   - a table: finding id/source (R-file + row) | document §/line | decision (accepted / rejected / partly) | change made | evidence `path:line`;
   - a "Requests for other documents" list;
   - a short summary of counts.
8. Do not git commit. Keep your final reply to the coordinator to 150 words or fewer.
