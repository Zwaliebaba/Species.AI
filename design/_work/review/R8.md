Three most serious findings:
1. **32 §6.3 (L371) says that "No such file ships in the tree" for `testbedconfig.txt`. This is wrong.** `SRC/testbedconfig.txt` exists: 14 lines, 4 commands, no final LF. 29 §11.15 and 31 §14.1 #9 describe it correctly.
2. **The AVI/Grabber reachability is misstated in 31.** 31 §3.12 (L435) says the Grabber window is "opened from the debug menu" and tags the path [B:DEBUG]. 31-U11 (L1635) then leaves reachability [U]. In fact `DebugKeyBindings::GrabberButton` has **no caller at all**: grep finds only `code/interface/debugmenu.h:33` and `debugmenu.cpp:401`. It is also not a `DebugMenu` row, and `DebugMenu` is never constructed. The AVI path is therefore dead in every build. 32 §3.2 #9 and §5.4 have this right.
3. **25 classifies `data/shapes/globalworld_{inner,middle,outer}.shp` as [B:LEGACY], "unreachable" (L3297–3299).** In fact they are loaded at every start-up: `App::Initialise` → `new GlobalWorld` (`code/app.cpp:454`) → `new SphereWorld` (`code/global_world.cpp:1408`) → `GetShape` (`global_world.cpp:875–877`), and a missing file is fatal (`code/lib/resource.cpp:573`). This contradicts 25's own §1.3 rule, which counts the start-up `TaskManagerInterfaceIcons` constructor pre-load as FULL.

Other notable findings:
- The language phrase counts in 28 §10.5 are raw entry lines; 29 §6.1 has the correct distinct counts.
- 31 §7.7 and 29 §4.3 call TankBattle "compiled out", which contradicts 01.
- Several deliverables cite non-deliverable `_work/` scripts and CSVs as their generation source or evidence (the Medium gaps).
