# X-DEC-P4b hand-off to X-DEC-P4a

Cross-file amendments that X-DEC-P4b needs in decision files owned by X-DEC-P4a. Each item gives the entry, the field, the exact text to replace and the exact new text. Replacements for `verdicts_and_fixes.md` are no longer than the text they replace (byte counts given), because that file has no room.
