## New DEC requests and DEC text fixes

1. **Empty candidate lists after exclusions (new Open DEC, or a note in DEC-107/DEC-014).** With DEC-107 a map can exclude every bad outcome, and any map can exclude every valid good outcome. Default written: the branch grants nothing and the crate is consumed (R-CRATE-003, R-CRATE-124, R-CRATE-126), matching G05's empty starting-power rule. Alternative: an empty bad pool falls through to the good branch.
2. **DEC-465 vs DEC-653 item 7 (wording conflict, both Resolved).** DEC-465 says equal weights at a zero total; DEC-653 item 7 says the drop "takes the unweighted choice directly", which reads like the random fallback. I applied DEC-465 and extended it to the p test as the equal-score limit: p = max(1 ÷ n, 1 − 1 ÷ n) (R-CRATE-106 step 2, AC-C-02). Please align DEC-653 item 7.
3. **DEC-550 airstrike wording.** DEC-550 says the airstrike Power "is marked running when it fires and is removed when its airstrike flight has gone"; ORIG 04 §5.5 says the airstrike Power terminates at once. I state the one-shot stop (README R-CRATE-017) and keep only "the airstrike Power is unaffected by the block" (R-CRATE-021). Please reconcile in DEC-550.
4. **Opening progress in integer units (note for DEC-002).** Progress is integer units (1 per Worker per tick, cap 50, full 2500); the crate opens when progress *exceeds* full. This reproduces the reference 51-tick minimum of DEC-021 and the reference counts for 1, 2, 5, 10, 25 and 50 + Workers; for 4 and 20 Workers it is one tick later than the reference's floating-point accumulation (626 and 126 ticks). Suggest adding "Supply crate opening 51 ticks" to the DEC-002 tick list.
5. **Neutral crate life = 600 ticks (confirm).** ORIG gives 60.0 s without the counter's precision; 600 ticks assumed (G00 also uses 600).
6. **Forced outcome makes no selection draw.** DEC-652 names only the 0.9 roll; I also skip the good-pool draw for a forced (tutorial) outcome (R-CRATE-121). Harmless under DEC-013; confirm or extend DEC-652.
7. **Shockwave active ticks.** Stated as A = ⌈10 × size⌉ − 1 (6, 12, 7, 24 and 2 ticks for grenade, bomb, rocket, meteor/barrage and Sickness, all equal to ORIG). For an exact-tenth size such as the Transport's death explosion (1.0 s) the reference's double countdown gives 10, the rule 9. Note for DEC-241 item 3.
8. **Rocket life ticks.** Stated as the first tick on which the age exceeds the nominal life: 3, 6, 8, 11, 16 ticks at levels 0–4 (8 or 9 moves at level 2, 11 or 12 at 1.0 s, as ORIG). Conflicts with G03b's `ref.bldg.turret.rocket.life_ticks` = 10 (see cross-document requests).
9. Known items already in `phase4_dec_fixes.md`: DEC-304 must say "every team" (I state global, R-CRATE-210); DEC-116 vs the start hold (R-CRATE-102 states both facts).
