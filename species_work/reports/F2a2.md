## Routing conventions applied

- **Paired rows on one range.** The gate dedupes identical ranges, so these share a range:
  - KEEP + TO-DEC (06 §3.10, §12.5; 07 §16; 09 §4.5, §11.13; 10 §5.2, §10.1, §13).
  - DROP-PROVENANCE `DP-CLOSING` + TO-DEC on every "Open questions" section, as `drop_topics.md` says.
  - KEEP + TO-ADR (06 §11.6), KEEP + DEFER-DESIGN (09 §4.4), MERGE + DEFER-DESIGN (07 §15) and TO-DEC + TO-ADR (08 §9).
  - One triple, KEEP + TO-DEC + DEFER-DESIGN, on 06 §13.
- **Line ranges finer than H3.** These keep map lists and names out of G08:
  - 08 §4.4–§4.7, §6.1, §6.3 and §6.5: each generated per-map table (`<!-- S08:... -->` blocks) is its own `TO-ASSETS` range, and the convention text around it stays `KEEP`.
  - 08 §7.3 is split four ways: KH-ACHIEVE text, the per-map id table (TO-ASSETS), and the content-entitlement remnant (DL-DEMO).
  - 09 §5.2: discovery (DD-LOBBY) and the directory message catalogue (DL-SERVICES).
  - 09 §7.4: statistics are KH-ACHIEVE, and the live rocket-mode status panel is KEEP.
  - 09 §9.4 and §9.6: the live parts are KEEP; the intro, demo-end and credits-loader parts are DL-CUT or DL-DEAD.
  - 10 lines 7–8: a units convention inside the H1 preamble is KEEP to G10.
- **Recommendations stay out of reference documents** (§4 rule 6). Every "re-implementation notes", "design notes" and "recommended for the new game" section is routed TO-DEC: 06 §14, 07 §20, 08 §9, 09 §13, 10 §8.8, §9.5, §14.1 and §14.4.
- **ORIG 08 map content.** No map list or map name is routed into a target as content. The per-map tables go to TO-ASSETS for map counts, thumbnails and availability. Hidden, test and debug maps are DL-TESTMAPS (§3.4, §7.2), and campaign files are DL-CAMPAIGN (§7.1). G08 KEEP notes say "no map names or map list in the target".
- **Reason classes that fit only approximately.** F1 may want to add a class for "compiled but unreachable, not campaign":
  - DL-DEAD for 06 §11.7 (input filters, never registered) and 09 §11.9 (key-binding window, no entry point).
  - DL-CUT for 09 §8.3 (objective tabs, computed but never drawn; the corpus calls this a cut feature). Confirm against ORIG 40 D10.
