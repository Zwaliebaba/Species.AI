## Readings that a reviewer should confirm

- **Barrier Emitter energy control:** "timed out: lower if raised or RelayOnly; otherwise raise if not raised" is the nested reading of the input. The flat reading would make a timed-out emitter rise again at once.
- **Turret idle sweep:** later searches *move* the aim height by U[−25, 25); the input does not say whether this sets or offsets the height.
- **Turret friendly-fire guard:** it applies to the flame turret only and blocks only automatic fire, because the input places it under the flame turret's automatic targeting.
- **Hive search kinds:** kind 2 takes the first enemy actor in result order ("any enemy entity"), and kind 0 or 1 the nearest enemy Worker ("nearest-listed").
- **Offspring height factor:** the input's formula gives 0.675 to 0.825 of the parent height; its own summary said 0.65 to 0.85, and I followed the formula.
