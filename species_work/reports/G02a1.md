## New DEC requests

1. **Game-speed divisors in Worker rules under tick scheduling.** The Worker fire roll (`NextUint mod ⌊10 ÷ s⌋`), the burning roll (`NextUnit(10) < 2.0 ÷ s`) and the carry lift radius (`20 × s × speed ratio`) read the game speed *s* (R-UNIT-007, R-UNIT-079, R-UNIT-082, R-UNIT-103). The documents keep the formulas, as DEC-653 item 5 assumes. Under DEC-039 every tick is a normal tick, so keeping ÷ *s* makes the attack attempts per game second proportional to *s* and burning damage per game second proportional to 1 ÷ *s* (the reference gave about 1 attempt per game second at any speed, and 4 ÷ *s*² health per game second). Options: A keep the formulas (stated default); B drop the ÷ *s* (1 attempt and 4 health per game second at every speed); C rescale to reproduce the reference per-game-second rates exactly. Decide by: design phase.
2. **Fire-damage kill over-count** (R-UNIT-119). Each flame-damage call after a Worker's fire damage reaches 100 adds one more kill to the attacker. Kept quirk (DEC-006); options: A keep (default), B count the kill once.
3. **Trooper per-actor rate under floating-point stats** (R-UNIT-014). With DEC-090 the Trooper's table rate 0.5 would become a real 0.5 s reload gate per Trooper and change the Strike team's fire rate. The README keeps two values: per-actor rate 0 and table rate 0.5 read by the Strike team. Please confirm as a Resolved entry or record it under DEC-090.
4. **Range of the Worker's Hive and Spitter Plant check** (R-UNIT-098 step 5). The corpus says only "in the Worker's obstacle cell, within range"; the document states "within its threat range". Open, default as written.
