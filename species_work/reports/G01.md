## New DEC requests

1. **Relic spawn window arithmetic.** The input does not say whether `60 × S / Z` and `window / 2` are exact or integer divisions. relic_run.md R-MODE-306 assumes exact (real-valued) division. The two readings differ only when Z does not divide 60 × S, or when the window is odd.
2. **The Flag Chain "under attack" status under DEC-078.** DEC-078 makes the under-attack status a live status, but it gives no trigger and no rank.
   - flag_chain.md R-MODE-462 sets it for an owned Home post with enemy Workers within its radius at the latest recount, which is the condition of the base-under-attack announcement.
   - It ranks the statuses: safe, under threat, unoccupied, under attack, enemy occupied.
   - Please confirm, or open a DEC.
3. **DEC-082 in Relic Run.** In the reference, a draw fallback (no qualifying player team) lets a faction with more than 50 actors win. Under DEC-082 this becomes a draw (relic_run.md R-MODE-307 step 4). Please add relic_run.md and launch_race.md to the DEC-082 "Affects" list.
4. **The repeated result declaration.** A second `DeclareResult` call adds the local kill total to the statistics hook again. This is kept as the rule (R-MODE-057) under DEC-047. It may deserve its own line in DEC-047.
5. **Multi-round Siege tick conversions** (unreachable, DEC-066). In the input, the fade-out starts at "victory timer ≤ 1 s" and the next-round countdown shows at "< 11 s". These are stated as `ref.mode.siege.fade_out_ticks` (10 ticks) and `ref.mode.siege.next_round_display` (110 ticks). The input gives no exact tick replay for them.
6. **DEC-017's option count.** DEC-017 says "39 option definitions". With the debug-only mode dropped, the six schemas hold **38**, and the README says 38. Please correct DEC-017 (and G00's mirror line).
