### Method

Helper scripts under `species_work/scripts/` (`R4a_extract.py`, `R4a_format.py`, `R4a_decls.py`, `R4a_registry.py`, `R4a_usage.py`, `R4a_ledger.py`, `R4a_layer.py`, `R4a_terms.py`, `R4a_schema_fields.py`; run with `python3 -I`, output in `species_work/scripts/R4a_out/`):

- extracted all 386 fenced blocks; the 30 C++ and HLSL sketches (29 `cpp`, 1 `hlsl`) were also read in full, the 69 untagged and 20 `text` pseudocode blocks and the 11 Mermaid class diagrams checked for identifiers;
- ran **clang-format 18.1.3** (the version CI pins) with the repository `.clang-format` over every sketch: 27 are a no-op, three drift (findings 31 to 33);
- parsed every declaration (1,159: 144 structs, 85 classes, 112 enumerations, 641 fields, 55 free functions, 10 aliases, 5 macros, 4 constants) and checked the `.clang-tidy` naming options (types, functions, enumerators, `_camelCase` parameters, `m_`/`sm_`/`g_`, `UPPER_CASE` constants and macros), R2 affixes, R4 acronyms, R10, R11 spellings, R12 COM lifetimes and R17 strings;
- parsed the CONVENTIONS §8 registry (936 names) and compared it with every declaration (layer, enumerators, duplicates) and with all 1,720 code-like backticked tokens in prose, including every `Type::Member` citation;
- compared the schema keys of every data-schema table whose caption names a C++ type with the fields of that type's sketch (ADR-062 generates `positionX` from `position_x`);
- listed every identifier of an `Engine` section that contains a game-concept word (CONVENTIONS §8 layer test);
- checked capitalisation variants of every multi-word term of §8.1 to §8.10, and every ledger identifier for use in the library.

### Checked and found consistent (no finding)

- Apart from the layout drift, every sketch passes the `.clang-tidy` naming rules: no parameter without `_`, no private member without `m_`, no non-`UPPER_CASE` constant or macro, no non-PascalCase type, function or enumerator; no `I`/`C`/`E`/`S` prefix, no `Base`/`Impl`/`Abstract` suffix, no `_t`; no British spelling in a code identifier; no `using namespace`; no raw `AddRef`/`Release`; no non-const `char*`; no reserved identifier form. All `.hlsl`/`.hlsli` names follow `<Shader><Permutation><Stage>.hlsl` and their permutation parts follow RND-261.
- Every `Type::Member` citation in prose matches its sketch, except `RoutePointKind::GroundPosition` (finding 5). The enumerators of `PlayerCommand` (30), `MapOption` (15), `MapEffect` (5), `EffectKind` (50), `ParticleKind` (17), `PageId`, `TimelineOp` and `DebugCommandKind` agree between the registry, the sketches and the owning tables; the map header fields of reference/08 table 2.6-A are the camelCase of the DAT table 3.2-A keys.
- No superseded ledger spelling survives in the library (`IronDefence`, `ColourRgba`, `MatchUI`, `RelicBase`, `UiButtonBase`, `UiPanelBase`, `kProductName`, `SetColour`, `OrbRain`, `ControlHints`: 0 uses). The ledger itself still lists several of these old forms; it is a run file, so that is not reported here.
- No `Engine` declaration names a game concept or a `Species` name other than those of findings 1, 12 to 15 and 36.
