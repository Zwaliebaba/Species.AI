**Method.** For every fact I read the sampled line in `design/` (with at most 10 lines around it where the line was a table row or a truncated sentence), looked up its routing row in `species_work/routing.csv` (helper `species_work/scripts/R1b_route.py`), translated the original names through `species_work/rename_map.tsv`, and searched `design_next/` by number and by Species term. A fact is `present-equal` only where the requirement, table row or tuning key named in the location column states the same number, rule, state or member; `present-changed-by-DEC` cites the DEC that records the change; `dropped-legacy-correct` names the reason class of `species_work/drop_topics.md` (provenance, legacy technology, campaign, dead, debug, platform, cut); `deferred-design-correct` names the `decisions/deferred/` entry. Header rows of ORIG tables count as present when the library carries the table they head. Per-source-file call counts, code-scan statistics and survey counts are classed as provenance (prompt §6.2, coverage counts), and noted where the per-site rule they support is present.

**Reading discipline.** Two searches went wider than the sampled lines: a keyword search of `design/22_determinism_and_networking.md` while checking row 270, and one of `design/24_rendering.md` while checking row 302. The extra lines they printed were not used for any classification or fix in this file.

**Patterns seen in the sample.** The engine-component facts (rows 281 to 480) are carried with unusual precision: every terrain vector value, audio effect constant, network-window layout figure and soak-report column checked here is in the library. The defects cluster where the library restates a reference behaviour in words rather than numbers: what the reference does during the grace period, how a no-object sound event is positioned, and what the reference explainer panel does on a screen change.
